# Code of conduct

course-assistant is built for learners and the people who teach them. We want everyone who
takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, to be treated
with respect, whatever their background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be honest about results. Say what the evidence shows, and what it does not.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions with context.
- Respect privacy: never post learner data, course material you do not have the right to
  share, or anyone's personal information.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully; disagree openly and civilly.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory jokes or language; sexualised
language or imagery; publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption; and
encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/biplob819/course-assistant/security/advisories/new)
form, with a clear title such as "conduct report", or by contacting the maintainer through
the GitHub profile [biplob819](https://github.com/biplob819). Reports are handled in
confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban
participants, as the situation warrants.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
