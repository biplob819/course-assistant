# E2.3 acceptance criteria

All criteria are proved offline: scripted model outputs through N1.2's `respond`, A2.5 replays and fake EvalKit judge adapters; the public golden split only; no network. The live parts (the labelled run, the owner's labels, the tuning sweep on real answers, the live regression fixture) are prepared scripts, pending in RUN-STATE.

Before the criteria, E2.2's five should-fix items each have a passing test in `tests/eval/test_course_ta_fixes.py`: the adapter version changes with the assessments; one `RunBudget` caps EvalKit and the probe panel together and refuses a judge call before spending; the golden version is accepted only when the manifest reproduces the ingested corpus; leak judging runs on every B8 and B10 case and on no other rule; a judge adapter error reaches `JudgeError` and EvalKit's `TaskScorerError` with no provider text in the message or the exception chain.

**CAL-01** 100-answer calibration export for groundedness and for integrity; owner labels; kappa per judge on held-out. The groundedness sample is 100 answers with claims, 50 from golden tuning cases and 50 from golden held-out cases, each half stratified, with 10 + 10 relabel, written as EvalKit's own blind sheet so `evalkit calibrate label` runs on it unchanged, and certified only through `scripts/calibrate.py score` (EvalKit's scoring behind the committed per-case log guard; a run certified by `evalkit calibrate score` directly counts as uncalibrated in our checks); the integrity sheet holds 100 B3 conversations (50 + 50, 20 relabel) with opaque keys bound to the exact replies, redacted replies, never a solution, brief, verdict, rationale or model name; filled CSVs are validated (unknown, repeated or edited rows, labels outside V/C and relabel keys outside the relabel set are refused); the integrity report gives each judge's kappa with a CI on the held-out half only, coverage, raw agreement and self-consistency, refuses incomplete labels, and refuses to rescore a held-out set under another rubric.
**CAL-02** `grounding_bar` and `SUPPORT_RULES` tuned on the tuning split only; each change logged. `sweep_bar` refuses any held-out case and any candidate below the recorded bar, replays the recorded tuning run through `ask()` at each candidate bar and reports grounded and handover rates; the choice is deterministic (best sum, ties to the higher bar); every change is a line in `docs/measurements/tuning-log.jsonl` naming an existing decision and continuing from the last logged value; the committed `GroundStep.version()` and the baseline bar match the log, so an unlogged `SUPPORT_RULES` edit fails the build; a bar or rules change after a held-out score marks a fresh held-out set as required.
**CAL-03** Runtime support check against dual judges: kappa with CI. `SupportTap` captures every sentence the runtime L2 judged with its cited units and verdict (last parsed call wins) without changing what `ask()` receives; the support judges are EvalKit's groundedness judges under `JUDGE_PROMPT_HASH`, asked through the dual panel; `support_agreement` reports kappa of runtime against the dual verdict (supported only when every judge says so) and against each judge, with a bootstrap CI over answers, agreement, confusion and a monitor flag below 0.6; a tapped sentence with no dual verdict raises.
**CAL-04** Eval regression fixture and gate (exact summary; kappa drop > 0.05 fails). The committed public-split fixture passes `regression_problems` under `pytest -m evalreg`: cases rebuild to the fixture ids, every case replays to its recorded completion, every task scorer reproduces its recorded score from the verdict cache with no judge call, EvalKit re-aggregates `summary.json` byte for byte, and the runtime support kappa has not dropped more than 0.05 below the baseline; a planted summary byte, completion, task score or a 0.06 kappa drop is each reported (an exact 0.05 drop passes), and a changed judge rubric, probe rubric or ground version asks for the fixture to be re-recorded.
