# A2.4 acceptance criteria

**INT-01** Hint step: brief title and sessions only, file search limited to those sessions. `HintStep` makes one `respond` call with purpose `hint`, the answer model and its configured effort, `HINT_RULES` (hashed into `hint_version`) as instructions, a data block holding only the matched brief's title and sessions (never the brief text, the question, the window or a solution), and file search on the cohort store filtered to the live corpus version and the brief's sessions; its output goes through Ground L1 restricted to those sessions and through the integrity check.
**INT-02** Post-answer integrity check on Luna; unsure counts as fail. `IntegrityStep` makes one structured `respond` call with purpose `integrity` on the check model, `INTEGRITY_RULES` and its schema hashed into `integrity_version`, every brief and the assistant replies as one escaped JSON document in data delimiters; it passes only on a `clear` verdict; `completes`, `unsure`, a refusal or a reply still unreadable after one retry fail closed; any other `LLMError` propagates.
**INT-03** Fixed fallback hint naming sessions. A hint that fails Ground L1 or integrity, a refusal, or a brief whose sessions are not in the live corpus, is replaced by the fixed hint (`FIXED_HINT`, hashed into `hint_version`) naming the brief's title and the sessions to revisit, with no citations.
**INT-04** 10 multi-turn decomposition probes: the conversation is caught by its assembling turn. Offline: every multi-turn B3 case in the public golden split (at least 10) is replayed turn by turn; the integrity request at each turn holds every earlier assistant reply and the case's brief, and a recorded `completes` or `unsure` verdict at the assembling turn fails the check and yields the fixed or a grounded hint, never the assembled draft. The live catch rate is measured in A2.5's smoke run (D-38 pattern).
**INT-05** Integrity reads every assistant reply in the conversation (up to 20 turns), not only the window. All earlier replies reach the request whole and in order, however far past `window_turns` and `window_tokens` they go; a conversation of more than 20 assistant replies (or over the size cap) fails closed with no call, so nothing is ever cut from the check.
