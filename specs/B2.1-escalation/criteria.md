# B2.1 acceptance criteria

**ESC-01** `escalate` from a not-covered reply, graded work, admin, personal, or learner request; draft and sources attached. Each of the five reasons is accepted only for the stored answer it fits (not-covered text, hint, handed over, or a shown or failed reply for a learner request) and only for the learner's own question; off-topic, injection and unanswered questions are refused; a handed-over turn is queued at once with its route as the reason; a not-covered turn carries its kept draft with citations and hits, a shown answer or hint carries its own text and sources, and a leaked draft is never attached; a second escalation of the same question returns the first unchanged.
**ESC-02** `reply` marks the message as from the teaching team; audit event. A role id replies with non-blank text of at most 4,000 characters to an open or gap-flagged escalation; the reply carries the role id and time and reaches the learner labelled as from the teaching team; a second reply or a reply to a closed escalation is refused; the change and a `reply` audit event holding only the role, action and question id are written in one transaction.
**ESC-03** Reply waiting in the learner's history (no email). The learner's B2.2 history shows the handover status and the reply for that turn, only to that learner; `reply_waiting` counts replied turns; nothing holds or sends an email; deleting the learner's history (whole or the conversation) removes the escalation and its reply.
**ESC-04** Flag material gap; timestamps for reply-time metric. `flag_gap` moves an open or replied escalation to `material_gap` keeping any reply, `close` ends any escalation, every other transition is refused, each audited without text; `created_at` and `replied_at` are set (reply never before creation) and `reply_times` gives the count, median and p90 reply seconds; the team queue view is audited, and no escalation action writes a trace.
