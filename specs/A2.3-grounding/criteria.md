# A2.3 acceptance criteria

**GRD-01** L1: citations resolve inside the filter; top score ≥ `grounding_bar`; no citation fails. Every citation and mark must be a unit of the live corpus (and of the picked session), with no unresolved citations, refusal or empty text; the top hit score must reach the configured bar; a failed L1 makes no support call and costs nothing.
**GRD-02** L2: Luna sentence support against cited unit text, outside knowledge not allowed. One `support` call on the check model sees each cited sentence with only the text of the units it cites; `SUPPORT_RULES` (with its schema and allow-list) is hashed into `ground_version`; an unparseable reply is retried once and then fails closed; any other `LLMError` propagates; the result satisfies the `GroundingResult` validator.
**GRD-03** Connective-sentence allow-list; uncited factual sentences fail. One uncited sentence per answer may exactly match the allow-list; any other uncited sentence is unsupported without a call; sentence splitting and mark-to-sentence mapping follow the spec.
**GRD-04** 10 planted common-knowledge questions come back `not_covered` (recorded). The public planted (B7) cases, answered from general knowledge with no citation, below the bar, or above the bar but unsupported, never pass; the live run of all 10 is part of A2.5's smoke run (D-38).
