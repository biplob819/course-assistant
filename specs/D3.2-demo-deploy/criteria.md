# D3.2 acceptance criteria

DEM-01 to DEM-03 are proved offline: Streamlit AppTest on the real entry point and pages, the
B2.5 and B2.3 rigs (D3.1's real gate and guard, scripted outputs through N1.2's `respond`,
file-backed stores), a D3.1 demo cache built from the golden fixture, and fakes; no network.
DEM-04 is the live deploy and is proved only by owner attest (`OWNER D-n`).

**DEM-01** Learner and teaching pages in `portfolio-demos`; replay default; live behind a passcode. `deploy/streamlit_app.py` routes exactly two pages, the B2.5 learner page (default) and the B2.3 teaching-team page, each behind its own passkey (the learner passkey never opens the staff page); with a demo cache loaded the learner page opens on "Worked examples" and shows stored answers with no model call, and both replay and live questions refuse without the passkey, own key or not; `build_kwargs` sends `max_tool_calls` equal to the executor's file-search bound on every file-search call, with the request model and golden replay unchanged.
**DEM-02** Secrets in the host secret store; aggregate-only logs. `.streamlit/secrets.toml.example` holds exactly the names (`OPENAI_API_KEY`, `DEMO_PASSKEY_HASH`, `STAFF_PASSKEY_HASH`, `DEMO_KV_REST_URL`, `DEMO_KV_REST_TOKEN`, `PID_SECRET`, optional `DEMO_TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS`) with empty values, and `.streamlit/secrets.toml` is git-ignored; the entry point serves only a locked page with fixed text when a required secret is missing, a hash is malformed, or the learner and staff hashes are equal; both pages read hashes through `LiveSecrets` at every check, so changing a hash in the host store revokes sessions with no restart; the passkey rate limit uses `client_ip`, which trusts only the configured number of proxy hops from the right of `X-Forwarded-For` (none by default); `deploy/requirements.txt` equals the core dependencies plus the `app` and `openai` extras; `.streamlit/config.toml` hides error details; no log record of a live turn, a refused unlock or a startup refusal holds a question, answer, passcode, hash, key, pid or IP.
**DEM-03** Off switch demonstrated live; nightly demo data reset. Offline: the staff "All sessions" pause on the shared store makes the learner's next live question return the paused answer with no model call, and resuming restores answers; removing `DEMO_PASSKEY_HASH` closes every page at the next check; `DailyStores` gives both pages one store per UTC day and removes earlier days' stores when the day changes. The live demonstration is part of DEM-04.
**DEM-04** Live deploy on Streamlit Community Cloud: the owner's app, repo connection and secrets; post-deploy smoke check passes (owner attest). The owner follows `docs/deploy.md`, runs `scripts/smoke_demo.py URL` (offline tests prove it passes only on a page showing the passkey input and nothing behind it), shows the pause switch and the passkey revocation working on the live app, and records `OWNER D-n` opening "Attests D3.2". Until then the row stays not green and D3.2 cannot flip green.
