# B2.3 acceptance criteria

All criteria are proved offline: a teaching-team passkey gate on its own hash (D3.1's real `PasskeyGate`), the learner chat rig of B2.5 (D3.1's gate and guard, scripted model outputs through N1.2's `respond`, a file-backed store and trace sink) to make real escalations and answers, B2.1's queue, digest rows written through N1.5's `Store.put_digest`, and Streamlit's `AppTest` for the page; no network.

Every criterion also holds these access rules: every `TeachingTeam` method except `unlock`, `sign_out` and `locked_reason` calls the staff gate's `require` first, before it reads the store, the corpus or the manifest (a test enumerates the public methods); the staff gate reads its own secret `STAFF_PASSKEY_HASH`, so the learner passkey and a learner's unlocked session never open it, a staff hash equal to the learner hash keeps the page closed, and a staff code that also opens the learner hash is refused; every view and action writes exactly one `AuditEvent` with the staff role id and as target a generated question id, a session scope (`s01`, ..., or `all`, for a pause or resume) or nothing, never text or a learner id (a test enumerates the views and actions); a store that will not open, or an audit that cannot be written, never crashes the page: the views and actions refuse with fixed text, the pause view shows every scope as paused, and unlock stays locked.

**TTV-01** Queue with question, reason, draft and closest sources; reply box. `queue` shows B2.1's queue (audited `view_queue`) as rows with the question, a readable reason and status (text icon plus text label), the kept draft and its sources as chips holding the cited unit's exact corpus text (or the closest search hits when the draft cites nothing), and no learner id; `reply` sends B2.1's reply (audited `reply`), which the learner then sees in their history labelled as from the teaching team; `flag_gap` and `close` are B2.1's (audited); a blank, too long or second reply is refused with a readable reason and nothing changes; on the page the reply box, flag and close buttons sit under each row and question, draft, reply and source text reach the page only through `st.text`.
**TTV-02** Digest tab. `digest(week)` shows the stored B2.4 rows of an ISO week (audited `view_digest`): session label, topic, distinct-learner count and flagged-gap count, never a pid or question text; a row below k (the larger of 5 and the configured `digest_k`) is never shown even if one reaches the store; an unknown week is refused; the page offers the current and earlier weeks and shows "No digest" for a week with no rows.
**TTV-03** Manifest view (read-only in the demo) with live corpus version. `manifest_view` shows the cohort, the live corpus version and one row per manifest entry (id, session, kind, path, licence, approver, approval date, speaker notes, units in the live corpus), plus the live units whose manifest id has no entry (a reconcile mismatch), audited `view_manifest`; the view has no method or widget that changes the manifest or the corpus.
**TTV-04** Pause switches per session and all, with confirm and audit. `pauses` lists "All sessions" and each live-corpus session with its own state, audited `view_pauses` (an unreadable state shows as paused, fail closed); `set_pause(scope, paused, confirmed=True)` writes N1.5's pause row and its `pause` or `resume` audit in one transaction, after which learners' `ask` gets the paused card for that scope; without the confirmation, for an unknown scope or without the staff passkey nothing is written; on the page each switch needs its confirm box ticked before its button is enabled.
**TTV-05** Ops tile: questions, status mix, handover share, p95, spend to date. `ops` (audited `view_ops`) counts stored questions, answers by status, the share of questions sent to the teaching team (escalated or handed over), the nearest-rank p95 of answer latency and the spend to date (the sum of stored answers' cost, plus an optional spend source such as the spend ledger), as numbers only; an empty store shows zeros and no p95; the page draws them as metrics with text labels.
